HemCard

Privacy policy

Effective October 18, 2026

HemCard is a manual garment-measurement photo-card app by Sepideh Javanbakht Dalir. It has no account, tracking SDK, advertising, backend or external AI service.

Your cards and photos

Your garment names, seller presentation choices, measurements and selected photo copies are stored locally in the app. HemCard does not upload them. Normal device backups may include app data. Removing the app removes its local library. Keep exported copies of important work.

Photo selection and exports

The system photo picker grants access to photos you select. HemCard does not request full-library, camera, location or microphone access. Imported photos are re-encoded from pixels. Generated card images do not copy original photo GPS/EXIF metadata. Copy listing text writes your chosen card's text to the system clipboard. When you share or save generated image/text files, your selected destination may receive that content under its own privacy practices. Deleting a card does not delete copies already exported or shared.

Purchases

Apple processes the optional Lifetime Pro purchase. HemCard reads product information and verified entitlement state to unlock features. It does not receive your payment card details or Apple Account password. Restore purchases uses Apple's StoreKit service.

Usage and support

Session-only event counts are kept in memory, contain no garment/photo/text data and are not sent to a server. No remote analytics or advertising profile is created. If you voluntarily email support, the owner receives the information you choose to send to answer your request.

Contact and changes

Questions or support requests: hello@arvinify.com. Material policy changes will be reflected here with an updated effective date.
